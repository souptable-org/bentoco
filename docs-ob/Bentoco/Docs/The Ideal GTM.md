In India, merchants don't trust software companies—they trust _their_ local guy. They trust the digital marketing agency running their Facebook ads in Thane, Pune, or Surat. If the agency says, "Move off Shopify," the merchant moves.

### The Strategic Pivot: Why Agencies Will Sell This For You

To make an agency sell your product, you have to understand their biggest nightmare: **Client Churn.**

Performance marketing agencies get fired when the client's Facebook Ads stop being profitable (ROAS drops). Right now, agencies are running great ads, but the client's profit is being wiped out by COD RTOs. The agency takes the blame and loses their ₹50,000/month retainer.

**Your Pitch to the Agency:**

> _"Stop letting Shopify's broken COD system ruin your ad performance. Move your clients to our engine. Our Pre-Paid Flip reduces RTOs by 30%. Your ROAS looks amazing, the client keeps paying your retainer, AND I will pay you 30% of their software subscription forever."_

### The Mechanics: Resolving the "Lifetime Deal" (LTD)

You mentioned selling a "Lifetime Deal" while also giving the agency a "recurring cut." Since your platform has variable costs (WhatsApp API messages), a traditional $99 one-time LTD will bankrupt you as they scale.

Here is the hybrid model that creates insane FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) for the merchant, while giving the agency recurring cash:

#### 1. The "Agency-Exclusive" Hook (For the Merchant)

You give the agency an exclusive offer they can use to close clients.

- **The Deal:** _"If you sign up through our agency, you get the Bharat Engine Founder’s Deal: A locked-in Lifetime Price of ₹2,999/month and a Lifetime Guarantee of 0% transaction fees. This deal is not available to the public."_
    
- **Why it works:** The merchant feels like the agency has elite insider access.
    

#### 2. The Recurring Bounty (For the Agency)

Shopify pays affiliates a standard 20% cut. You beat them by making it a true partnership.

- **The Cut:** You give the agency **30% recurring revenue** on the base subscription for the lifetime of the client, PLUS a **10% cut of the WhatsApp Wallet top-ups**.
    
- **The Math:** If an agency brings you 20 clients, they are making a passive ₹20,000 to ₹30,000 a month just from your software payouts, plus they protected their own marketing retainers.
    

### The "Boots on the Ground" Playbook (Your Execution)

Since you don't want to do direct sales, your only job is selling the _agencies_. Here is your 3-step operation:

#### Step 1: The Lead Arbitrage

You scrape BuiltWith or Wappalyzer for every Shopify store in India doing decent traffic. You don't cold call the merchants. Instead, you segment the list by city. You reach out to a mid-tier digital marketing agency in Mumbai and say:

> _"I have a list of 15 Shopify brands in Mumbai that are bleeding money on RTOs. I don't do sales. If I give you this lead list, and you migrate them to the Bharat Engine, you keep 30% of their subscription revenue forever. Want the list?"_

#### Step 2: The "God Mode" Agency Dashboard

Agencies hate Shopify because they have to manage 15 different client logins, 2-factor authentications, and app permissions.

You build a single **"Partner Dashboard."** The agency logs in once and sees all their clients' stores, daily sales, and RTO stats in one master grid. You make their operational life 10x easier than Shopify does.

#### Step 3: The Integration Bribe

When an agency agrees to partner, you do the heavy lifting for their first client. You tell them, _"Give me your most annoying Shopify client. My team will use our 1-Click Importer to migrate their entire store to a staging link by tomorrow morning for free. Show it to them. If they like it, you take the credit and the commission."_
