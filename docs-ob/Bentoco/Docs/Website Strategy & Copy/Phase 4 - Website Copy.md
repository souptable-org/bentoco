### Section 5: The "Pre-Paid Flip" RTO Defense Showcase

> **Section Header:** 🛡️ How BentoCo Stops Cash-on-Delivery Shipping Losses
>
> 1. **Cart Submission:** Buyer selects Cash-on-Delivery at checkout.
> 2. **Order Paused (`PENDING_OTP`):** Inventory is held, but the order is locked until verification.
> 3. **WhatsApp OTP & Pre-Paid Incentive:** BentoCo sends an automated WhatsApp message with a 4-digit OTP—and offers an instant 10% UPI discount trigger ("Pay via UPI now & save ₹150").
> 4. **Prepaid Conversion (`PREPAID_FLIPPED`):** 35% of COD buyers tap the UPI link to pay instantly, turning a high-risk COD order into a non-refundable prepaid sale.
> 5. **COD Verification (`COD_VERIFIED`):** Buyers who prefer COD enter the 4-digit OTP, filtering out fake addresses and bad intent before shipping labels print.
